CLINIC APPOINTMENT POLICY

1. Appointment Booking

--- CHUNK 2 ---
Source: clinic_appointment_policy.txt
Chunk index: 1

Patients can request an appointment through the clinic reception desk.
Patients may also request an appointment through the patient portal.
Each appointment must be linked to one patient and one doctor.
Staff should verify the doctor's available consultation slot before confirming a booking.

--- CHUNK 3 ---
Source: clinic_appointment_policy.txt

2. Cancellation Policy

--- CHUNK 4 ---
Source: clinic_appointment_policy.txt
Chunk index: 3

Patients should cancel unwanted appointments as early as possible.
The clinic recommends cancelling at least 24 hours before the appointment.
A missed appointment means the patient did not attend as expected.
Patients who cannot attend should contact the clinic before the scheduled time.

--- CHUNK 5 ---
Source: clinic_appointment_policy.txt
Chunk index: 4

3. Rescheduling

Patients may request a different appointment date when available.
Staff should verify the current appointment status before rescheduling.
Appointment status changes should be recorded consistently.

4. Records and Privacy

--- CHUNK 6 ---
Source: clinic_appointment_policy.txt

4. Records and Privacy

Only authorized staff should access confidential patient information.
Authentication is required for protected clinic records.
Patient records should be handled according to clinic privacy procedures.

CLINIC STAFF GUIDELINES

1. Doctor Schedules

Doctor schedules contain available consultation slots.
Staff must check schedule availability before confirming an appointment.
A doctor should not have two confirmed appointments in the same slot.

2. Appointment Search

--- CHUNK 11 ---
Source: clinic_staff_guidelines.txt
Chunk index: 1

2. Appointment Search

Search can help staff find appointments by patient name.
Search can also filter appointments by doctor.
The clinic may support department-based appointment search.

3. Appointment Changes

--- CHUNK 12 ---
Source: clinic_staff_guidelines.txt

3. Appointment Changes

Staff should verify the current status before changing an appointment.
The clinic may maintain an audit record for appointment changes.
Changes should include the time of the update and the responsible staff member.

4. Patient Communication

--- CHUNK 13 ---
Source: clinic_staff_guidelines.txt

4. Patient Communication

Patients should receive clear information about appointment changes.
Staff should communicate cancellation and rescheduling procedures clearly.
